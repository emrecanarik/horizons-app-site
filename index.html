Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your device. Horizons has no account and no server.

How do I move my data to a new device? Settings → Data → Export CSV on the old device, then Import CSV on the new one. Your device backup also includes it.

Reminders don't arrive. Check that notifications are allowed for Horizons in Settings, and that the horizon's reminder is switched on in Horizons → Settings → Reminders. A reminder only arrives if the next period isn't planned yet.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Data → Delete All Data, or delete the app.
