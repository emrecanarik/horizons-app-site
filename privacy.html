Horizons Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Horizons is built to collect nothing. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Data we collect

None. Horizons has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it makes no network requests. We cannot see your focuses, notes or reflections.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (focuses, notes, reflections, life-area names and settings) is stored only on your device, inside the app and its widgets. A weekly CSV backup is saved to the app's own folder on the same device. Uninstalling the app removes this data. You can also erase it at any time with Settings → Delete All Data.

Exporting and importing

Data leaves your device only when you choose to export it, using the system share sheet or file picker. Where it goes from there is up to you and governed by whichever app or service you send it to.

Notifications

Planning reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push service or server is involved.

Device backups

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, your device's backup may include Horizons data under Apple's or your own control. We never have access to it.

Children

Horizons collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
